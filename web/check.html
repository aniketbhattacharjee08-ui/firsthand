    What the reading means

    1. Is this GPTZero?

      No. It is a local estimate trained on GPTZero's own verdicts for several thousand texts. It agrees with GPTZero most of the time and costs nothing, but it is not GPTZero. Check a final draft with the detector your reader uses.

    2. What does the percentage mean?

      The probability a detector assigns to the whole text having been generated. Above about 65% most detectors say AI; below about 35% they say human; between the two, mixed. Texts under forty words are not scored, because no detector is reliable on them.

    3. What if my writing is flagged?

      Detectors react to the even cadence, stock transitions and hedged summaries that generated prose shares, and a person's writing can have them too. Vervly rewrites the draft in plainer, more particular sentences, keeps every fact, and shows the reading before and after.
