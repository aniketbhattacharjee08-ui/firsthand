What it does to a paragraph

Before

aiexample

The relationship between urban green space and public health has become a pivotal area of academic inquiry. Researchers across a range of disciplines have begun to delve into the mechanisms that connect vegetation cover to wellbeing. Moreover, this work underscores a broader shift in how modern cities are planned, managed and understood. It is important to note that this shift is multifaceted, ongoing and highly context dependent.

After

humanexample

Whether city parks make people healthier is now a real research question, not a slogan. People in several fields have tried to work out how tree cover ends up changing how someone feels. The interest says something about cities themselves: they are being planned and run differently than they were. None of this is settled, and what holds in one city may not hold in the next.

Both paragraphs were written by hand for this page, so the readings are examples of what the app shows, not measurements. In the app, any fact you add in your notes is the only new material a rewrite may use; a version that invents a name or a figure is thrown out before you see it.

Made in Berkeley

Vervly is built by a student at UC Berkeley, for people who write for a grade and get read by a machine first. It started as research: dozens of ways of rewriting a paragraph were measured against detectors before one of them was shipped, and the method still changes when the measurements do.

The gold is California Gold, the hairlines are Berkeley Blue, and the type is the campus pair, Source Serif for what you read and Inter for the controls. That is the whole tribute. There is no seal, no mascot and no claim on the university; the work is one student's, and the footer says so.
