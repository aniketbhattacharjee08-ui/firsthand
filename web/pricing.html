How it works

  • Words are counted on what you submit, before the rewrite runs.
  • A rewrite that comes back unchanged is refunded.
  • Unused words carry over, but only up to one month's amount.
  • Cancel any time from the Manage subscription button.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. Vervly never sees card numbers.
