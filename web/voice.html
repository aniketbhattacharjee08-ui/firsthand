How it works

  • Your samples become the examples the model continues from, in place of the stock ones.
  • Among rewrites that already pass the detector, the one closest to your habits wins.
  • A thumbs up or down after a run teaches it which samples to lean on.
  • Nothing the model writes is ever added to your samples.
